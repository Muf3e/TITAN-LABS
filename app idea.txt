What Is a Business Domain?

A Business Domain is a major area of organizational responsibility that owns a specific set of business capabilities, knowledge, data, workflows, policies, and decisions.

A domain is not a department.

Departments can change.

Domains remain stable.

23.3 Platform Design Principles

The platform must follow these rules:

Shared services must have clearly defined ownership.
Domain services must not directly depend on platform implementation details.
Platform capabilities must expose stable contracts.
Platform services must be independently deployable where practical.
Platform failures must not unnecessarily cascade across the enterprise.
Platform services must be observable.
Platform services must enforce tenant isolation.
Platform services must support versioning.
Platform services must support policy enforcement.
Platform services must be usable by humans, applications, workflows, and AI agents.
