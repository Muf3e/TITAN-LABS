Which rating would you actually trust?

TITAN Labs is testing one narrow question before building anything else: is an evidence-weighted product score more useful than a plain star rating? You'll see 10 real laptops and phones. For each, two rating systems are shown, labeled blindly as A and B.

  1. For each product, read both rating systems — you won't be told which is which until the end.
  2. Pick the one you'd trust more if you were actually about to buy the product.
  3. Say briefly why, in your own words.
  4. After all 10, you'll see which system was which, plus the live results across everyone who's taken the test.
